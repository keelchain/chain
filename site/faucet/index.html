Rules

Enough to build with, not enough to hoard.

1

One claim a day

Per address, and a handful per network address. The exact numbers are in the stats above; they come from the faucet's own configuration.

2

Two assets

KEEL pays for action budgets and offer deposits; KUSD is the chain's one-to-one dollar, the quote asset of every market and most P2P offers.

3

No value, ever

The testnet resets between releases; balances and claims reset with it. A client's own operator account is funded through onboarding, not here.

4

Scripted

POST https://testnet.keelchain.com/api/v1/faucet with {"address":"…"}; GET /v1/faucet for amounts and balances, GET /v1/faucet/claims for history.
